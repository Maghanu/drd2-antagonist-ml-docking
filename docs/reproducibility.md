## Receptor used by the checked-in docking workflow

[`Group6_codeACMDD_step06_docking_6CM4.ipynb`](../src/Group6_codeACMDD_step06_docking_6CM4.ipynb)
reads the receptor from the committed local
`data/processed/crystal_structures/6CM4_Na.mmcif`; it performs no structure
download. It retains DRD2 `ATOM` records at author residue numbers 35–222 and
364–442, excluding the T4 lysozyme fusion and unrelated records. The Na+ ion
from this processed file is included in the final Vina receptor PDBQT. PDBFixer
and restrained OpenMM minimization are applied to protein atoms; the sodium
is added to the PDBQT after that preparation, rather than being minimized or
parameterized by the protein force field.

Because the processed receptor file omits risperidone, notebook 06 reads
`data/raw/crystal_structures/6cm4.mmcif` locally only to get the native
risperidone (component `8NU`) coordinates for the docking box. The box has 8 Å
padding in each dimension. Both files are committed inputs. The prepared
receptor cache is `docking/prepared/6CM4_Na/`.

Suggested Methods wording for analyses produced by the 6CM4 docking notebook:

> The DRD2 receptor was prepared from the local processed 6CM4_Na mmCIF file.
> DRD2 `ATOM` records at author residue numbers 35–222 and 364–442 were
> retained, excluding the T4 lysozyme fusion. The structure's Na+ ion was
> included in the final receptor PDBQT after protein preparation. Native
> risperidone (component 8NU) coordinates from the local raw 6CM4 mmCIF were
> used only to define the docking box, with 8 Å padding. Missing protein atoms
> were rebuilt with PDBFixer, the protein was minimized with positional
> restraints in OpenMM, and Meeko generated the protein receptor PDBQT.

The report and its Figure 4 are not present in this repository, and
`docking/runs/docking_summary.csv` contains only its header. Thus the exact
receptor and procedures behind Figure 4, and whether the report's control
docking results were generated by the current workflow, cannot be verified
here. A separate notebook docks the named comparison compounds against
6CM4_Na, but this does not establish the provenance of historical report
results. Use the Methods wording above only if Figure 4 was generated from
the current notebook workflow. Existing docking scores and figures should be
regenerated before describing them as results from this Na+-containing
receptor.

## Active- versus inactive-state DRD2 docking

[`Group6_codeACMDD_step06_active.ipynb`](../src/Group6_codeACMDD_step06_active.ipynb)
implements a paired docking comparison for the five selected hits and
risperidone. It selects hit ranks 1–5 and the risperidone reference from
`data/raw/5ht2c_docking_ligands.csv`; it does not use the Ritanserin row in
that input.

The inactive receptor is the prepared local 6CM4_Na structure described
above. For the active receptor, the notebook reads the local
`docking/prepared/Active/9BS9.cif`, extracts DRD2 author chain R at residue
ranges 35–222 and 364–442, repairs missing atoms with PDBFixer, and prepares
the receptor PDBQT with Meeko. It uses native risperidone (component `8NU`) in
the local raw 6CM4 mmCIF and native LSD (component `7LD`) in 9BS9 to set the
two receptor-specific box centers. Both searches use a 24 Å cubic box,
AutoDock Vina's `vina` scoring function, exhaustiveness 16, 10 poses, and
random seed 22. The score saved for each run is the first pose's Vina score.
The comparison delta is `active 9BS9 − inactive 6CM4_Na`; negative values mean
the active receptor scored more favorably in these runs.

The notebook writes receptor-specific scores to
`results/tables/drd2_active_inactive_top5_vina_scores.csv`, paired scores and
deltas to `results/tables/drd2_active_inactive_top5_vina_comparison.csv`, and
poses and logs under `docking/runs/drd2_active_inactive_top5/`. The active
receptor preparation is cached in `docking/prepared/Active/9BS9_DRD2/`.
These Vina scores are estimates, not experimental affinities or evidence of
agonism or antagonism. The two crystal structures differ in conformation,
construct, co-crystallized ligand, and resolved components, so their score
difference is hypothesis-generating and should not be interpreted as an
isolated measure of receptor-state preference.

## Scope of implementation

The repository contains separate notebooks for the main prediction and
docking workflow, named reference-compound docking against 6CM4_Na, paired
active/inactive DRD2 docking of five selected hits plus risperidone, and
selected-ligand docking against 5-HT2C (6BQH). The paired 9BS9/6CM4_Na
workflow and its outputs are described above. These implementations do not
establish that historical report analyses or figures were generated by the
same procedures; in particular, molecular-dynamics analyses are not
reproducible from the checked-in workflows. The report and Figure 4 are not
present, so their exact inputs and procedures cannot be verified here.

The available scaffold collection is the result of a similarity search. A
report should call it a scaffold-similarity search unless scaffold novelty is
demonstrated with explicit structural comparisons; the search label alone does
not establish scaffold hopping.
